Agent Overtime - Art credits

The art in this folder is NOT under the MIT license that covers the code.
See LICENSE.txt in this folder.

The PNG files are unmodified copies from LPC Revised by Eliza Wyatt and
contributors: https://github.com/ElizaWy/LPC (each folder there has a
Credits.txt; the entries below are copied from them word for word).
Licensed OGA-BY 3.0. The app crops these sheets, stacks the character
layers, moves a head down one pixel for nodding, and darkens and tints the
picture at night while it draws; the files themselves are not changed.
