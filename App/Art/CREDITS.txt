Two packs, both kept as unmodified PNG files:
- The room and its furniture (Structure/, Objects/): LPC Revised,
  OGA-BY 3.0. Listed first.

  CC0 1.0. Listed after LPC Revised.

The LPC Revised files are copies from LPC Revised by Eliza Wyatt and
contributors: https://github.com/ElizaWy/LPC (each folder there has a
Credits.txt; the entries below are copied from them word for word).
Licensed OGA-BY 3.0. The app crops these sheets and darkens and tints the
picture at night while it draws; the files themselves are not changed.

MetroCity Free Top Down Character Pack (the people)
https://jik-a-4.itch.io/metrocity-free-topdown-character-pack
================================================================

MetroCity Free Top Down Character Pack by JIK-A-4, CC0 1.0 (no attribution
required, credited anyway): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The files under MetroCity/ keep the pack's own folders; the ones under
MetroCity/MetroCity 2.0/ come from its MetroCity 2.0 update.

-- Used here:
   MetroCity/CharacterModel/Character Model.png (bodies, six skin tones)
   MetroCity/CharacterModel/Shadow.png
   MetroCity/Hair/Hairs.png
   MetroCity/Outfits/Outfit1.png, Outfit5.png, Outfit6.png
   MetroCity/MetroCity 2.0/Hair.png, Suit.png, Suit1.png
MODIFIED: by Agent Overtime contributors, 2026, while drawing; the files
   themselves are not changed. The app stacks a body, clothes and hair;
   seats people by cutting the back view under the shoulders and drawing a
   chair's backrest over it, or, facing the room, by cutting the first
   front view at the chest and drawing a desk and laptop in front of it;
   moves that cut down one pixel for nodding;
   draws each pixel 1.5 times the size of the room's pixels; and moves the
   colours a little toward the LPC Revised palette (near-black outlines one
   step lighter, slightly less saturation and contrast).
